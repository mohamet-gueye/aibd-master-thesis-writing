\chapter{Appendices}
\label{chap:appendices}

The appendices provide documentary support for the methodological procedures, analytical results, simulation outputs, and decision-support artifacts presented in the main body of the thesis. Their purpose is to strengthen transparency, traceability, and reproducibility without introducing new arguments or extending the interpretation beyond Chapters 4 to 6. Detailed visualizations, complete Scenario comparisons, dashboard pages, and implementation extracts are placed here where their level of detail would interrupt the analytical flow of the main chapters.

All numerical values, thresholds, labels, and model outputs reproduced in these appendices are taken directly from the validated datasets, notebook outputs, AnyLogic exports, and Power BI files produced during the study. No value has been recalculated, rounded in a manner that changes its meaning, or replaced with an illustrative substitute. Where a figure or table also appears in the main text, the cross-reference is stated so that the relationship between the summary presentation and the complete evidence remains explicit.

The appendix structure is as follows: Appendix A contains the simulation figures; Appendix B contains the pressure analysis and forecasting figures; Appendix C contains the analytical and Scenario tables; and Appendix D contains the code and implementation excerpts. The five pages of the Power BI Decision Support System are presented in the main text, in \Cref{sec:decision-support-system}.
