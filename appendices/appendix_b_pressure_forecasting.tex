\chapter{Pressure and Forecasting Figures}
\label{app:pressure-forecasting-figures}

Appendix B contains the complete analytical and forecasting record derived from the validated commercial-arrival dataset. Figures B.1 to B.3 document demand estimation and the pressure profiles; Figures B.4 to B.6 report the passenger-pressure regression; Figures B.7 to B.11 report the congestion classification, presented in two groups so that the effect of the information leakage diagnosed in \Cref{subsec:time-based-validation} remains visible; Figures B.12 to B.14 report the explainability analysis. The main text carries only the leakage evidence and the corrected performance curve (Chapter 4, Figures 4.1 and 4.2; Chapter 5, Figure 5.2).

\section{Congestion classification: full-feature specification}
\label{sec:appendix-b-full-feature-classification}

The figures in this subsection report the full-feature specification in which
\nolinkurl{future_baggage_pressure} was retained as a predictor. They are
affected by the information leakage diagnosed in Chapter 4,
\Cref{subsec:time-based-validation}, and are reproduced for methodological
transparency rather than as performance estimates.

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[
        width=\textwidth,
        height=0.78\textheight,
        keepaspectratio
    ]{figures/appendix_b/fig_b_7_congestion_class_distribution.png}
    \caption{Class distribution of the
    \texttt{is\_high\_congestion} target in
    \texttt{ml\_dataset.csv}: 14,636 records (73.5 percent) labeled as
    normal conditions and 5,271 records (26.5 percent) as high congestion.
    This imbalance motivated stratified sampling in the train and test
    split. Source:
    \texttt{notebooks/forecasting/06\_congestion\_classification.ipynb}.}
    \label{fig:appendix-b-congestion-class-distribution}
